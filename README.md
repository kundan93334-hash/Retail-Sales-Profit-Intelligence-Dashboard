🎯 Business Problem
🎯 Business Problem
High sales do not always mean high profit. This analysis looks at how discounts, products, regions, and customers affect profitability and where profit is being lost.

🔎 Key Insights
Discount Impact: Discounts above 20% were associated with negative profit in some Furniture sub-categories.
Profitability: Overall profit margin was approximately 14.7%, showing the need to track profit alongside sales.
Customer Value: Some repeat customers placed 25+ orders, but order frequency did not always translate into higher profit contribution.
Sales vs Profit: Category and regional performance showed that strong revenue does not necessarily mean strong profitability.
💡 Business Recommendations
Review high-discount products generating low or negative profit.
Avoid blanket discounts on low-margin products.
Use targeted offers or product bundles instead of heavy discounts.
Monitor Sales + Profit + Discount together in regular reporting.
